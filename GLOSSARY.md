Translate ordinary `связь` contextually as connection, relationship, or link. `связность` is generally connectivity in the system analysis. Do not collapse those into Coupling, which names Kael's Ability. Likewise, `опорный` may be reference in a named technique and requires context elsewhere.

## Signatures and Abilities

All formal names below first appear in I.3. A Signature and its associated Ability are separate entries, not synonyms.

| Character | Russian Signature | English Signature | Russian Ability | English Ability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ollan Veir | Незамкнутый Контур | Open Circuit | Шунт | Shunt |
| Ilsa Merr | Соматическое Созвучие | Somatic Resonance | Компенсация | Compensation |
| Taven Ker | Метрическая Тень | Metric Shadow | Опорная Сетка | Reference Grid |
| Kael Reven | Сопряжённая Последовательность | Coupled Sequence | Сцепление | Coupling |
| Mirael Tessen | Опорное Состояние | Reference State | Якорь | Anchor |

Limits stated in I.3, to protect against translation drift:

- Shunt temporarily bridges disrupted mana connectivity through Ollan's own soul channel; it is not simply increased power.
- Compensation can stabilise physiological/mana imbalances and buy time. The text explicitly denies effortless wound healing or resurrection.
- Reference Grid establishes a local standard of measurement; it does not simply repair spatial distortion. “The Grid” is an established contextual short form.
- Coupling traces dependencies in a sufficiently understood system. It is neither prophecy nor infallible access to truth; uncertainty and overload matter.
- Anchor establishes selected current states as references and increases resistance to interference. It does not confer invulnerability.

These notes are translation checks, not an exhaustive magic-system specification. New source evidence can refine them.

## Recurring warning

I.3 presents the instruction as an inscription; I.4 recalls it in dialogue/narration:

> Если Узел снова начнёт считаться седьмым — не искать восьмой.

Published I.4 wording:

> If the Node begins to be counted as the seventh again—do not look for the eighth.

Retain the counting/recognition ambiguity. Match the actual source wording and inscription capitalisation when repeating it; do not paraphrase it into a definitive instruction about physical travel alone.
